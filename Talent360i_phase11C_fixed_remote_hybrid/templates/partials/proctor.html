Before you start

  • Stay on this page{{ ', in full screen,' if view.fullscreen }} until you submit. Switching to another tab, window or app{{ ', exiting full screen' if view.fullscreen }}, or closing, reloading or leaving the page is a strike.
  • One screen only. Disconnect any extra monitor before you start. A second display is detected and is a strike.

  • Your own words: no AI tools, no other people. How each answer is typed (characters, corrections, pauses) is recorded, and answers are checked for AI-written wording.
Strikes and penalties
  • Every strike shows a warning. At {{ view.max_strikes }} strikes your attempt is submitted automatically, as it stands.
  • Medium risk (any strike, 15 s or more away, repeated blocked actions): {{ view.medium_points }} points off each skill's MCQ score in the recommendation.
  • High risk (the strike limit, a second display, or a minute away in total): your manager is recommended to send the attempt back, and a retake opens {{ view.cool_off_days }} days after their decision. A second high-risk attempt within 180 days is reported to HR.

Only what happens on this page during the assessment is recorded, with times. There is no camera, microphone or screen recording. Your manager sees the record and makes the decision.
